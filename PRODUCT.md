## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** Homeowners, office managers, and facility owners in Sri Lanka evaluating air-conditioning suppliers for domestic, commercial, or industrial installations.
- **Secondary:** Contractors and project managers sourcing branded AC units and ongoing maintenance for client sites.
- **Tertiary:** Returning customers booking service calls, gas refills, or breakdown repairs.

Most visitors arrive via mobile, often on 3G/4G connections, and need to reach a phone number, brand list, or sector solution within one or two taps.

## Product Purpose

Celsius exists to be the single trusted supplier-installer-maintainer of premium air conditioning systems across Sri Lanka. Success means: a visitor can identify the right sector solution (Domestic / Commercial / Industrial), recognise the brands Celsius carries, verify credibility through case studies and client list, and reach the team by phone or email within one session.

## Positioning

Unlike generic appliance retailers, Celsius is an authorised supplier of nine premium brands (Midea, Daikin, Panasonic, Haier, Mitsubishi, Samsung, TCL, Chigo, LG) with end-to-end ownership — supply, installation, scheduled maintenance, and breakdown repair. The differentiator is full lifecycle accountability: the same team that sells the unit also keeps it running.

## Product Principles

1. **Cooling is engineering, not commodity.** Every page should reinforce precision: BTU sizing tables, sector-specific solutions, brand-by-brand rationale, not generic "we sell AC" copy.
2. **Trust before pitch.** Lead with proof (40+ clients, 9 brands, since 2019, real case studies) before asking for the call.
3. **Phone-first.** The phone number and Book Demo CTA must be reachable from any scroll position within one tap.
4. **Slow-wifi safe.** Site must remain usable on 3G: lazy-load heavy assets (WebGL, video), fallback for every animation, no content hidden behind JS-dependent opacity.
5. **One warm accent.** Amber is the only warm colour — use sparingly to draw the eye to CTAs, key numbers, and interactive affordances.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text against navy and off-white surfaces.
- Reduced-motion users: skip loader, skip entrance animations, show all content immediately.
- Keyboard: full mobile-menu operability via Escape to close, focus-visible rings on all interactive elements.
- Touch targets: minimum 44×44px (current burger button is 44×44).
